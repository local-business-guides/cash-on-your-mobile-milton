Ask about the full repayment

A fast application does not replace checking the details. Before agreeing, get clear answers about the total amount you would repay, any fees or other costs, the repayment dates and what happens if a payment is late. Make sure you understand how the loan fits alongside rent or mortgage payments, utilities, food and other regular costs.

Keep this short checklist handy

Before you speak with a loan provider, prepare the basics and note your questions:

  • The expense you need to cover and the amount involved.
  • The date the money is needed and your expected next pay date.
  • A simple view of regular income and household outgoings.
  • Questions about repayment amounts, timing, costs and late payments.
  • Your personal or business bank account details, if requested through the application process.

Only provide information through a channel you know belongs to the business. Don’t guess at figures or agree to anything just to move quickly. A careful check can help you spot a repayment that would leave too little for essentials.
